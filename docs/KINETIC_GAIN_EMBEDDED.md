# Kinetic Gain Embedded

Board Approval Capacity Brief is designed to open an embedded executive-intelligence engagement:

- convert reviewer bandwidth, committee load, and backlog drag into explicit board-visible staffing tradeoffs
- expose where hiring or reallocation can recover decision speed before another lane is funded
- tie escalation coverage and decision coverage to named operating surfaces instead of abstract process complaints
- convert scattered approval bottlenecks into one reusable intervention packet for committees and investors
